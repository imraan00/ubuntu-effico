About us

Ubuntu Effico Design Consulting (Pty) Ltd is a B-BBEE Level 1 consulting firm with 135% procurement recognition, founded by Mr Yeshodhan Moodley, a purpose driven Civil Engineering Technologist, in the year 2022.

We had humble beginnings and began as a drafting service to traffic and civil engineers.

We have grown significantly and now work alongside industry specialists to provide trusted solutions to our clients.
